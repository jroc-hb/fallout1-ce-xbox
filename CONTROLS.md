# Controls

Fallout CE for the Original Xbox is played entirely with the controller. This page lists every
control. Settings mentioned here are in **XBOX OPTS** on the main menu.

## Two ways to move: walk mode and pointer mode

Click the **left stick** to switch between them at any time outside combat. The message box
says which one is on ("Controls: walk with the left stick." / "Controls: pointer."), and the
choice is remembered.

- **Walk mode**: the **left stick walks your character** directly (push lightly to walk,
  all the way to run). The camera follows you. The pointer is hidden, and whatever usable
  thing is next to you is highlighted in gold with a label such as "Talk: Slummer" or
  "Take: 10mm Pistol". Press **A** to use it.
- **Pointer mode**: Fallout's normal point-and-click. One stick moves the pointer, the other
  pans the camera. By default the pointer is on the left stick. **SWAP STICKS** in XBOX OPTS
  puts it on the right.

Combat always uses the pointer (see [Combat](#combat)). With walk mode on, the pointer is on
the right stick and the left stick pans the camera.

## Seeing which button does what

- **Hold LT** to show a controller button icon on every interface bar button that has a
  shortcut (they fade in as you squeeze the trigger): Y, X, START (options), Black (item mode), RS (Skilldex: the right stick click
  opens the skill wheel), BACK (map, character and Pip-Boy are in the menus wheel), and White
  by END TURN / END COMBAT in combat. They go away when you let go. Holding LT also outlines
  everything usable on the map.
- In **walk mode**, the label over the highlighted object starts with **A**
  (e.g. **(A)** Talk: Slummer).
- The **wheels** show **(A)** next to what choosing does and **(B)** Close.

| Input | Walk mode | Pointer mode | Combat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Left stick** | Walk / run | Pointer (camera if swapped) | Camera in walk mode; otherwise as in pointer mode |
| **Right stick** | Shows the pointer and moves it | Camera (pointer if swapped) | Pointer in walk mode; otherwise as in pointer mode |
| **A** or **RT** | Use the highlighted object. Hold: its action menu | Click. Hold: action menu | Click (attack, move) |
| **B** | Look at the highlighted object | Right click (change cursor type) | Right click (change cursor type) |
| **X** | Inventory | Inventory | Tap: reload. Hold: inventory |
| **Y** | Swap hands | Swap hands | Swap hands |
| **Black** | Change weapon mode | Change weapon mode | Change weapon mode (single, burst, aimed, reload) |
| **White** | — | — | Tap: end turn. Hold: end combat |
| **LT** (hold) | Outline everything usable on screen | Slow, precise pointer + outline everything usable | Slow, precise pointer + outline everything usable |
| **LT + d-pad** | Skill shortcuts (below) | Skill shortcuts | Skill shortcuts |
| **D-pad** | Switch the highlight between nearby objects | Jump the pointer to the next object in that direction | Jump the pointer to the next character in that direction |
| **Left stick click** | Switch to pointer mode | Switch to walk mode | Centre the camera on your character |
| **Right stick click** | Skill wheel | Skill wheel | Skill wheel |
| **Back** | Menus wheel | Menus wheel | Menus wheel |
| **Start** | Options menu | Options menu | Options menu |

"Tap" means a quick press. "Hold" means keeping the button down for a moment (under half a
second).

## Walk mode in detail

- **What gets highlighted**: the nearest person, item, door or usable object within two
  steps, preferring what you're facing. Things behind a wall aren't picked.
- **A (tap)** does what clicking the object would: talk, pick up, open, search or use.
- **A (hold)** opens Fallout's action menu on it (look, talk, use an item on it, use a skill,
  and so on). Pick an entry with the **d-pad** or the **right stick** and **let go of A** to
  choose it.
- **B** looks at the highlighted object.
- **D-pad** moves the highlight to the next object in that direction. Your pick stays until
  you move.
- **Walking into a closed door** opens it.
- **Pull the stick the other way** to turn around straight away, even mid-step.
- **Touch the right stick** to bring the pointer back, for example to click something far
  away. It appears just in front of your character, and A clicks normally again until you
  walk.
- In conversations, the inventory and other menus, **both sticks move the pointer** and the
  camera stays still.

## The wheels

Both wheels pop up around your character. Point the **left stick** at an entry (or step
through them with the **d-pad**), press **A** to choose it, and **B** to close the wheel. The
middle of the wheel says what A will do.

### Skill wheel (right stick click)

The eight Skilldex skills, each with your percentage:

| Position | Skill |
|---|---|
| Up | Sneak |
| Up-right | Lockpick |
| Right | Steal |
| Down-right | Traps |
| Down | First Aid |
| Down-left | Doctor |
| Left | Science |
| Up-left | Repair |

The skill is used straight away on the highlighted object (walk mode) or on what's under the
pointer (pointer mode), for example "A: on Slummer". First Aid and Doctor fall back to
yourself. Sneak just turns on or off. If there's nothing to use the skill on, you get the
skill cursor to pick a target with the pointer.

The **LT + d-pad** shortcuts use the same layout: hold LT and press the direction of the
skill (for diagonals, press both directions).

## Combat

Combat is turn-based and uses the pointer in both modes: aim with the pointer stick and press
**A** or **RT** to attack the enemy under it, or to move to a spot.

- **D-pad**: jump the pointer to the next character in that direction.
- **B**: change the cursor type (move, attack, look).
- **X (tap)**: reload the weapon in your active hand. This costs action points as usual.
- **Black**: change weapon mode, for example single shot, burst or aimed shot.
- **Y**: swap hands.
- **White (tap)**: end your turn. **White (hold)**: end combat when no enemies are left.
- **Left stick click**: centre the camera on your character.

## Menus and conversations

The pointer works everywhere: **A** clicks and **B** right-clicks. In conversations, **Y**
opens Barter. In XBOX OPTS, the d-pad moves between settings and changes them, **Start**
applies and **Back** cancels. **Start** also backs out of the character selection screen.

## Related settings (XBOX OPTS)

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Swap sticks** | Pointer on the right stick, camera on the left (pointer mode) |
| **Pointer assist** | The pointer slows over things you can use and bends toward them. **+RUMBLE** also gives a small rumble when it reaches one |
| **Pan centers cursor** | Panning the camera keeps the pointer in the middle of the screen |
| **Edge scroll** | Pushing the pointer against the screen edge scrolls the map |
| **Pointer / camera deadzone and sensitivity** | How far a stick must move before it counts, and how fast it goes |
| **Rumble** | Rumble strength, 0 turns it off |
